      Open your Model 12 SD card

      Put the card in your computer, click Open SD card… and pick the card (usually named TASCAM_M) or a copy of it.

      The app only reads from the card. Nothing is uploaded: everything stays on your computer.

              Clear tracks

              Empties tracks of this song on the card, for example everything except 7/8 to start a new recording on top of a bounced mix.

              Tick all except

                Trim song

                Cuts the same stretch off the start and end of every track, so they stay in sync. Each track keeps its own Model 12 header and its audio is copied sample for sample: nothing else changes.

                  Import audio

                  Puts an audio file on an empty track, like TRACK IMPORT on the Model 12. Mono goes on a single track, stereo on a pair. WAV, FLAC, MP3, M4A/AAC, OGG and Opus; other sample rates are converted to the song's.

                      Export stereo mix

                      Makes one stereo file of the song.
